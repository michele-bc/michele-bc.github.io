Co-organized the Workshop: 3D Spectroscopy of Galaxies held at the Institute of Astronomy, Geophysics and Atmospheric Sciences (IAG/USP). The event aimed to train undergraduate and graduate students in modern Integral Field Spectroscopy (IFS) techniques. My role involved organizing the event structure, preparing diverse material and guiding participants through STARLIGHT software to analyze stellar populations.

Workshop site found [here]([https://sites.google.com/view/workshop-ifu/](https://sites.google.com/view/workshop-ifu/)) and git [here]([https://github.com/kefrankk/spectroscopy-workshop](https://github.com/kefrankk/spectroscopy-workshop)).
